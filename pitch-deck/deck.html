Why Now

Three trends collided in 2025.

01

Multimodal AI is good enough.

Gemini 3 Flash identifies food from a photo with ~90% accuracy at 12s median latency. Two years ago this was a research demo. Today it's a $0.003 API call.

02

On-device models arrived.

iOS 18's Foundation Models framework runs recipe generation free on-device. No per-request LLM bill on the most expensive feature in the app.

03

Cal AI proved demand — and ceiling.

Cal AI hit $20M ARR in 8 months doing photo-only. They also got called out for ±30% calorie errors. The market wants this fixed. We fix it.

Defensibility

A data moat that compounds.

USDA lookup is table stakes. The real moat is five AI operations that only work with corrected user data — sharper every week you use it, impossible for a new entrant to clone cold.

01

Personal portion calibration

Learns per-user offsets from corrections. "Zach's chicken portions run 15% over USDA" — silently adjusts the next time.

02

Brand & restaurant routing

Photo cues detect a Big Mac vs. a generic cheeseburger. Routes to chain nutrition DBs when it matters.

03

Context inference

Home plate vs. restaurant vs. takeout. Modifies portion estimate before the number hits your screen.

04

Cross-meal anomaly detection

Flags predictions >2σ from your 30-day baseline before they ruin your trend line.

05

Temporal freshness

Aggregated corrections across users keep the nutrition DB current when USDA lags by years.
